Google has announced Genie 4, an updated world model that adds integrated audio generation and real-time interactive steering capabilities. The model can maintain consistent scene rendering for up to two minutes, doubling the threshold of its predecessor model Genie 3. Google reported improvements in physics simulation and character rendering within generated environments.
